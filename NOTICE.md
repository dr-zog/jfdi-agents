## Cucumber and the Gherkin language

This plugin's agents produce and consume [Gherkin](https://cucumber.io/docs/gherkin/)
`.feature` files and delegate test execution to a Cucumber-family runner
(cucumber-js, cucumber-ruby, SpecFlow, pytest-bdd, behave, etc.) chosen by the
Architect on a per-project basis. The plugin does not redistribute any Cucumber
or Gherkin source. The specific runner a downstream project installs is covered
by that runner's own licence.
